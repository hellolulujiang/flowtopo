# Notice on the bundled data

The MIT terms in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) cover the code and the documentation in this
repository: `flowtopo/`, `tests/`, `examples/`, `docs/`, and the scripts at the top
level. They do not cover the files in `data/`.

`data/dir_example.tif` is an excerpt of MERIT Hydro, 292 rows by 614 columns at
3 arc-seconds (Yamazaki et al., 2019,
<https://doi.org/10.1029/2019WR024873>), which its authors distribute under
CC BY-NC 4.0. It is redistributed here under those terms, for reproducibility,
with attribution to its authors. `data/bsn_example.geojson` and
`data/outlet_example.geojson` are the basin boundary and the outlet derived from
it, and carry the same terms.
